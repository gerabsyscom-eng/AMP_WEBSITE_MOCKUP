A portfolio built for real projects, not a catalogue

A value-added distributor sits between the manufacturer and the channel. AMPCONNECT holds the stock, the vendor accreditation and the technical knowledge, so resellers, system integrators and consultants can design and deliver a project without managing a separate relationship with every vendor.

Every brand in this portfolio was selected because it completes a solution rather than duplicating one. Cabling, networking, enclosures, power, computing, security and monitoring interlock — which is why most projects draw on more than one vendor from this page.
