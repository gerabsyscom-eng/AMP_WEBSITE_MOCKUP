What we do

One partner for the entire cabling plant

AMPCONNECT is a value-added enterprise cabling distributor serving the UAE, GCC and Africa. We supply structured copper and fibre cabling systems, connectivity and cable management for offices, campuses and datacenters, from CommScope, Datacom, Leviton and Optronics Plus, held as regional stock in JAFZA, Dubai.

As a distributor behind the channel, we give contractors, integrators and IT teams the products, pre-sales design and BOQ support to deliver certified, warrantied cabling infrastructure at any scale.

What we distribute

Copper and fibre cabling, end to end

From the outlet to the datacenter backbone, we distribute the products that make up a complete, certified structured cabling system.

Copper Structured Cabling

Cat6, Cat6A and higher copper systems, keystones and patch cords for high-speed LAN connectivity.

Fibre Optic Cabling

Single-mode and multimode fibre, MPO and MTP trunks and high-density fibre for backbone and datacenter links.

Patch Panels & Frames

Copper and fibre patch panels, racks and frames for organised, serviceable termination.

Connectivity & Modules

Jacks, connectors, adapters and outlets that complete the channel end to end.

Cable Management & Pathways

Trays, ducts and managers that keep the cabling plant tidy, cooled and maintainable.

Warranty & Certification

Guidance on certified installations and manufacturer warranty for compliant infrastructure.
