Who we are

Transforming IT distribution since 1987

AMPCONNECT is a fast-growing technology distribution company in the region, enabling business partners to operate more efficiently and successfully in the markets they serve.

Since 1987, our local experts have transformed the IT components and passive networking solutions marketplace with high-quality products that deliver reliable, effective and effortless connections.

We specialize in structured cabling solutions and an ISO-certified value-added distributorship of passive networking products, OEM and multi-tech partner IT components throughout the UAE, GCC and Africa.
