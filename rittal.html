About Rittal

Global Innovation, Trusted Delivery

Rittal is a global manufacturer of industrial and IT enclosures, racks, cooling solutions and power management systems — founded in Germany in 1961 and now operating in over 90 countries. As the largest enclosure manufacturer in the world, Rittal provides innovative, high-quality infrastructure solutions for practically any industrial or IT application, from a single rack to a complete, mission-critical data centre system.

Network & IT Enclosures

Rittal TS IT Server Rack

The 19-inch backbone of any IT installation

The Rittal TS IT server rack is the 19-inch backbone of any IT installation — from single-rack deployments to fully configured enterprise data centres. Engineered to provide a stable, controlled environment for sensitive electronics, it integrates seamlessly with Rittal's complete ecosystem of cooling, power, monitoring and security accessories.

The portfolio extends from open frameless designs for maximum accessibility to fully enclosed racks with dust and water protection — always matched to the existing or planned cooling strategy, whether room climate control, double floor or rack-level climate control.

Compact IT Enclosure

Rittal's Compact IT Enclosure delivers a space-saving 19-inch solution for small network applications — designed for flexible use as either a wall-mounted or floor-standing enclosure. Network & IT Enclosures

Power Distribution & CMC III Monitoring

From basic PDUs to socket-level intelligent monitoring

Rittal offers a complete range of power distribution units — from basic PDUs for low-consumption environments to intelligent PDUs with individual socket-level monitoring and control for energy demand management. The CMC III monitoring system communicates with sensors and actuators via an integrated CAN bus, integrating seamlessly into standard DCIM platforms for real-time visibility across all physical IT parameters.

CMC III sets new standards in configuration simplicity and reduced system complexity — delivering immediate alarm notification for any deviation from set environmental parameters and ensuring continuous, autonomous infrastructure oversight.

RiMatrix IT Container

Ready-to-connect IT infrastructure in a robust container shell

The RiMatrix IT Container delivers standardised, pre-assembled IT infrastructure modules in a robust container shell — available in a range of output categories for SME, cloud and edge applications. Delivered fully pre-assembled and ready to connect, it is the ideal solution for organisations facing space constraints, rapid deployment requirements or temporary infrastructure needs.

Data Centre Container

RiMatrix Power & Cooling Container

Modular, redundant UPS for containerized data centers

The RiMatrix Power Container provides modular, redundant UPS systems designed to supply one or more IT modules within a containerised data centre environment — ensuring continuous power availability for critical workloads regardless of site conditions. The RiMatrix Cooling Container pairs with it to deliver efficient recooling in a pre-assembled container frame, featuring redundant chillers with integral or external free cooler options.

Both solutions are delivered ready-to-connect and fully system-tested, with integrated network functions for remote monitoring and management — making them ideal for outdoor deployment in demanding climate conditions across the GCC.

Edge Data Centre

Rittal Edge Data Centre

Flexible, low-latency IT infrastructure at the network edge

The Rittal Edge Data Centre delivers high computing power and reliability at the point where data is generated — eliminating the latency of centralised cloud processing for real-time applications in manufacturing, logistics, healthcare and smart buildings. Available in configurations of 2, 4, 6 or 8 racks, it combines predefined power, cooling, IT security and monitoring components into a ready-to-use solution in minimal time.

Available as a fully virtualised private cloud — customer-managed or as a fully managed service — with cover options ranging from a basic enclosure to a full IT security room or outdoor container for flexible location deployment.

RiMatrix Micro Data Centre

The all-in-one OT solution for decentralised IT infrastructure

The RiMatrix Micro Data Centre unites all relevant system pillars in a single bundle — rack, power, cooling, monitoring and security — in a globally standardised, individually configurable platform. Covering all output categories and adapting readily to any ambient conditions, it is designed to scale as requirements grow without complex upgrades.

IT Cooling

Blue e+ Cooling Units

Energy-efficient rack cooling from 1.6 kW to 5.8 kW

Rittal's Blue e+ cooling units deliver indoor climate control for small to medium rack applications using patented hybrid cooling technology — achieving average energy savings of up to 75% compared to conventional cooling units. Precise-fit and easy to install with no pipework required, Blue e+ units are available in multiple rack sizes and cooling classes.

Ideal for edge locations, branch offices and distributed IT deployments where installation simplicity and energy efficiency are the primary requirements, Blue e+ is the market-leading choice for rack-level cooling in the lower output range.

IT Cooling

Liquid Cooling Unit (LCU) & Liquid Cooling Package (LCP)

In-rack liquid cooling with no loss of floor space

The Liquid Cooling Unit (LCU) integrates directly into the rack to deliver highly efficient cooling for medium IT applications from 3 kW to 6.5 kW — with no loss of floor space and minimal carbon footprint. For larger heat loads, the Liquid Cooling Package (LCP) delivers inline, closed-loop row cooling from 12 kW to 53 kW, available in DX (air/refrigerant) and CW (air/water) variants with output that adjusts dynamically to capacity utilisation.

Compatible with hot and cold aisle containment, the LCP delivers high cooling performance covering 1 to 4 racks per unit — making it the preferred solution for medium to large enterprise data centre and colocation environments across the UAE and GCC.

IT Cooling

Precision Climate Control — HPC & Large Data Centres

Guaranteed temperature and humidity control at scale

For high-performance computing (HPC) environments and large-scale data centres, Rittal's precision climate control range delivers guaranteed temperature and humidity control accuracy across 45 unit options — with cooling outputs from 3 kW to 481 kW. The portfolio includes precision climate control units, turnkey cooling containers and atmospherically sealed chiller systems.

Demand-based cooling provides the foundation for optimum energy efficiency across the entire data centre. Compatible with cold and hot aisle containment, Rittal's HPC cooling range ensures consistent separation of hot and cold air to reduce operating costs and maximise infrastructure reliability at scale.
