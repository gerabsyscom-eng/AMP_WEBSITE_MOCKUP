Privacy Policy

AMPCONNECT is a value-added IT distributor serving resellers, system integrators and technology vendors across the UAE, GCC and Africa. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have over it.

Section 01

Introduction and scope

This Privacy Policy describes how AMPCONNECT handles personal data. It applies when you visit ampconnectme.com, submit an enquiry or quotation request, register for an event or webinar, deal with us as a reseller, system integrator, vendor or supplier contact, apply for a role with us, or otherwise correspond with our teams by email, phone or WhatsApp.

AMPCONNECT is a business-to-business distributor. In practice, most of the personal data we hold is business contact data about individuals acting in a professional capacity — a name, job title, work email address and work phone number — rather than data about consumers.

This policy does not apply to third-party websites, vendor portals or marketplaces that you reach through links on our site. Those services publish their own privacy notices.

Section 02

Who we are

AMPCONNECT is an ISO-certified value-added IT distributor and a Sharafi Holding Group company, headquartered in the Jebel Ali Free Zone, Dubai, with further offices in Garhoud, Dubai and in Muscat, Sultanate of Oman.

For the purposes of the UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (the PDPL) and, where it applies to our activities, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (the GDPR), AMPCONNECT acts as the controller of the personal data described in this policy. Where we process data on behalf of a vendor or customer under a written agreement, we act as a processor and follow that party’s documented instructions.

Our full contact details are in Section 16.

Section 03

Information we collect

a. Information you give us directly

  • Identity and contact details — full name, job title, company name, work email address, work telephone or mobile number, and business address.
  • Enquiry content — the products, brands, quantities or solutions you ask about, project details you choose to share, and the content of your messages to us.
  • Trading and account information — trade licence and registration details, VAT or tax identifiers, credit application information, authorised signatories, and purchase order or delivery contacts.
  • Event and campaign data — registrations for our vendor events, training sessions and webinars, and any dietary or accessibility requirements you volunteer for an in-person event.
  • Recruitment data — your CV, work history, qualifications, references and right-to-work information when you apply for a role.

b. Information we collect automatically

  • Technical data — IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, screen size and language settings.
  • Usage data — pages viewed, time on page, referring and exit URLs, downloads, and the approximate region derived from your IP address.
  • Cookie identifiers set by us or by the analytics and marketing tools described in Section 5.

c. Information we receive from others

  • Technology vendors and OEM principals, for deal registration, project approvals, warranty and support administration.
  • Resellers, system integrators and end customers, where they name you as a project, delivery or technical contact.
  • Credit reference, compliance, sanctions-screening and anti-money-laundering providers, where we are required to carry out due diligence.
  • Logistics, freight-forwarding and customs partners handling shipments.
  • Publicly available business sources, including company websites, trade registries and professional networks such as LinkedIn.

d. Data we ask you not to send us

We do not seek out special categories of personal data — such as health, religious or political information — and we ask that you do not include it in enquiries or correspondence. If you send it to us unprompted, we will delete it unless we are required to retain it by law.

Section 05

Cookies and tracking

Cookies are small files placed on your device when you visit a website. We use them in the following broad categories:

  • Strictly necessary — required for the site to load, to keep it secure and to remember your cookie choices. These cannot be switched off.
  • Functional — remember preferences such as language or a form you part-completed.
  • Analytics — help us understand which pages and solutions are of interest, so we can improve the site. These are used for aggregated measurement, not to identify you personally.
  • Marketing — used to measure campaign performance and, where applicable, to show relevant AMPCONNECT content on third-party platforms.

Non-essential cookies are set only where you have given consent through our cookie banner, where one is displayed. You can change or withdraw that consent at any time through the banner, and you can block or delete cookies through your browser settings — though parts of the site may then not work as intended.

Our site does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals, as no common standard for them has been agreed.

Section 06

How we share information

We share personal data only where there is a clear reason to, and only with:

  • Group companies — other entities within Sharafi Holding Group, for administration, shared services and internal reporting.
  • Technology vendors and OEM principals — for deal and project registration, pricing approvals, licensing, warranty and technical support.
  • Logistics and trade partners — freight forwarders, couriers, customs brokers and warehousing providers who deliver your orders.
  • Service providers — IT, cloud hosting, CRM, email, marketing and support platforms that operate our systems under contract.
  • Professional advisers and insurers — auditors, lawyers, bankers and credit insurers, where required.
  • Authorities — regulators, courts, customs and law-enforcement bodies, where we are legally obliged to disclose or need to protect our rights.
  • A buyer or successor — if AMPCONNECT is involved in a merger, acquisition or transfer of part of its business, subject to appropriate confidentiality protections.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not rent or trade our contact databases to third parties for their own marketing.

Every service provider we use is bound by contract to process personal data only on our instructions, to keep it confidential, and to apply appropriate security measures.

Section 07

International transfers

We operate across the UAE, the wider GCC and Africa, and many of the vendors and platforms we work with are headquartered elsewhere. Your personal data may therefore be transferred to, or accessed from, countries outside the one in which you are based.

Where we transfer personal data across borders, we do so in line with the conditions set out in the UAE PDPL and, where the GDPR applies, on the basis of an adequacy decision, Standard Contractual Clauses, or another approved safeguard — together with contractual confidentiality and security commitments from the recipient.

You can ask us for more detail about the safeguards applied to a specific transfer by contacting us at info@ampconnectme.com.

Section 08

How long we keep it

We keep personal data only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, and then delete it or anonymise it. When we decide how long that is, we look at:

  • how long the trading, supplier or employment relationship is active;
  • statutory retention periods for accounting, tax, customs, export control and corporate records in the UAE and Oman;
  • the limitation periods during which a legal claim could still be brought; and
  • whether the data is still needed to answer a question you may reasonably ask us later, such as a warranty query.

Marketing contact data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to stop, after which we retain only a minimal suppression record so that we do not contact you again by mistake. Unsuccessful job applications are held for a limited period so we can consider you for future roles, unless you ask us to delete them sooner.

Section 09

How we protect it

As an ISO-certified distributor, we apply organisational and technical controls proportionate to the data we hold. These include role-based access controls and the principle of least privilege, encryption of data in transit, network segmentation and perimeter security, endpoint protection, logging and monitoring, secure backup, staff awareness training, and due diligence on the vendors who handle data for us.

We maintain an incident response process, and where a personal data breach is likely to prejudice the privacy, confidentiality or security of your data, we will notify the competent authority — and you, where required — without undue delay.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please do not send passwords, full payment card numbers or other highly sensitive information to us by unencrypted email.

Section 10

Your rights and choices

Subject to the conditions and exemptions in applicable law, you have the following rights over your personal data:

AccessAsk what personal data we hold about you and receive a copy of it.
RectificationHave inaccurate or out-of-date data corrected or completed.
ErasureAsk us to delete data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep.
RestrictionAsk us to pause processing while a concern is investigated.
PortabilityReceive certain data in a structured, machine-readable format, or have it sent to another controller.
ObjectionObject to processing based on our legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time.
Stop processingRequest that we cease processing your data where the law allows.
Withdraw consentWithdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@ampconnectme.com with the words “Data Request” in the subject line. We may ask you for information to confirm your identity before we act, and we will respond within one month, or within any other period required by applicable law. If we need longer because the request is complex, we will tell you why.

Exercising these rights is free of charge. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the UAE Data Office or, where the GDPR applies to you, to the data protection supervisory authority in your country. We would appreciate the chance to resolve the matter with you first.

Section 11

Marketing preferences

Where we send you product news, vendor announcements, event invitations or technical content, every message includes an unsubscribe link. You can also write to info@ampconnectme.com at any time and ask us to stop.

Opting out of marketing does not stop transactional and service messages — order confirmations, delivery notices, support responses, invoices and similar communications — which we need to send in order to do business with you.

Section 12

Children’s privacy

Our website and services are intended for business use by professionals. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Section 13

Automated decisions and AI

We do not make decisions that produce legal effects, or similarly significant effects, about you based solely on automated processing without human involvement. Credit and account decisions are reviewed by our team before they take effect.

We do use automation in limited, routine ways — for example spam and malware filtering, fraud and security monitoring, and tools that help our teams draft or summarise correspondence. Where any such tool is used, output that affects you is reviewed by a person. If we introduce automated decision-making that requires notice under applicable law, we will update this policy and tell you about your right to request human review.

Section 14

Third-party websites

Our site links to vendor websites, product datasheets, partner portals and social media pages that we do not control. Following those links means the third party’s own privacy notice and cookie practices apply. We are not responsible for their content or their handling of your data, and we encourage you to read their notices.

Section 15

Changes to this policy

We review this policy at least once a year and update it when our practices, our services or the law change. The current version always appears on this page, with the date it was last updated shown at the top.

Where a change materially affects how we use your personal data, we will give you reasonable notice by email or through a prominent notice on the site before it takes effect.

Section 16

Contact us

If you have a question about this policy, want to exercise a right over your data, or wish to raise a concern about how we have handled it, please get in touch. We aim to acknowledge every privacy enquiry promptly.
